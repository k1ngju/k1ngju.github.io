    What "distance" means here

    Straight-line (geodesic) distance from the summit to the nearest point of Slovenian territory — which for these peaks is the north-west tip of Slovenia at the Austria–Italy–Slovenia tripoint, or the Bovec area further south-west. Not driving distance.

    Side tops

    OpenStreetMap records many subsidiary tops. A peak is marked side top when a higher summit lies within 1.2 km — a rough stand-in for prominence, not an official criterion.
