The honest constraint

Small and cheap barely coexist anymore

Here's the trap your search keeps hitting. The phones that are actually small (6.1–6.2") are old flagships — Galaxy S23, the discontinued Asus Zenfone — and they hold their price near 450–500 €. The phones that are actually cheap (under 300 €) are all 6.5–6.9" slabs — the Redmi 16 you flagged, the whole Galaxy A-line, every Redmi Note. There is almost nothing that is both.

The one real escape hatch is the 6.36" class — mainly Motorola's Edge Neo line. It's meaningfully narrower than your current A15 (6.5"), light (~174 g), and lands at 250–330 € with a chip that will crush Discord + YouTube ReVanced + Gmail without breaking a sweat. That's why it's the pick. Below is where every candidate falls on the two axes you actually care about.

The takeaway: battery isn't what's failing you

Your A15 posts 14.5 hours of real endurance — genuinely good, and better than the Galaxy S23 or the Pixels. So the "constantly dying" feeling is almost certainly a worn-out cell and the chip thrashing under load, not a small battery. Every green pick here matches or beats it on longevity, and the two things that actually change day-to-day are: charge speed — the Motorola picks refill in ~40 min at 68W vs the A15's ~1h10 and the Xperia/Pixel-8a's painful 1h40–1h53 — and a fresh battery that hasn't lost 15% of its capacity to two years of cycles. Net: don't pick on endurance (they're all fine), pick on size + smoothness, and enjoy the faster charging as a bonus.

If I had to hand you one decision

Buy the Motorola Edge 60 Neo (or the 50 Neo to save ~50 €). It's the only thing that satisfies small-ish + cheap + smooth + low-bloat simultaneously, and either one will feel like a different universe from the A15. Only override that if one axis is absolute: if "tiny" truly outranks everything → hunt a used Pixel 8a (6.1", clean) or stretch to a Galaxy S23; if "never see bloat again" outranks everything → Pixel 9a and keep it seven years. And whatever you buy, your ReVanced/Discord/office load is light enough that any phone on the green rows will stay smooth well into the 2030s.
